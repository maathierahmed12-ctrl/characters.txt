Elara decided she wouldn’t face the Whispering Woods alone again—and more importantly, she didn’t have to. The next time she returned to the forest’s edge, Shadow was already there waiting, half-hidden beneath the twisted roots of an old oak as if he had always belonged to the place. He didn’t announce himself or explain anything; he simply fell into step beside her when she started forward, moving with a quiet ease that made the forest seem less hostile and more like a space he understood in ways she didn’t. At first, Elara was wary of him—his silence felt deliberate, his presence too calm—but as the path deepened and the strange rustling returned, she found herself listening less to the woods and more to him. Shadow didn’t speak much, yet his steady presence made the unknown feel less overwhelming, as if whatever danger or mystery waited ahead would have to reckon with the fact that she was no longer facing it alone.
