Elara hesitated at the tree line, then stepped into the Whispering Woods, where the world seemed to shift its breath. The air was cooler here, heavy with the scent of damp earth, crushed leaves, and something faintly floral that felt almost unfamiliar. Towering trees arched overhead like ancient sentinels, their intertwined branches filtering the daylight into soft, flickering patterns that moved even when the wind did not. Every step she took sank slightly into a carpet of moss and fallen needles, muffling her sound until even her own presence felt uncertain. A low, constant rustling drifted through the forest—too rhythmic to be random wind, too distant to be clearly alive—and it made her pause more than once, listening as if the woods might answer back. Despite a thread of unease coiling in her chest, Elara also felt a quiet pull of wonder, as though she had crossed into a place that had been waiting for her long before she arrived.
