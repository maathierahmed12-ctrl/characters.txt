Elara spread the map across the wooden table in her small cottage, smoothing its curled edges with careful hands. The parchment felt unnaturally warm, and faint lines seemed to shift when she looked away. Symbols marked places she had never heard of—spiraling trees, a river shaped like a serpent, and a single marked path leading straight into the Whispering Woods. As she traced the ink with her finger, she noticed something strange: the map subtly pulsed beneath her touch, as if responding to her presence.

That night, sleep eluded her. The wind outside her window carried faint whispers, just as the elders had warned. By dawn, Elara had made her choice. She packed a small satchel with bread, a flask of water, and a worn lantern, then tucked the map safely inside her cloak. With one final glance at her village, she stepped beyond its edge and into the looming forest. The trees seemed to lean closer, as though recognizing her arrival.
