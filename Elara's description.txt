In the quiet edge of a forgotten valley, where mist lingers between ancient trees, a young villager stumbles upon a secret that will change everything. Elara has never ventured far beyond her home, yet her life shifts the moment she uncovers a strange, timeworn map buried beneath the roots of an old oak. Its ink seems to shimmer faintly, as though alive, tracing paths that lead deep into the forbidden heart of the Whispering Woods. Locals speak of voices in the wind and paths that move when unobserved. Now, Elara must decide whether to follow its call—or leave mysteries undisturbed.
